# Code of Conduct

Guess Karo is a place to learn, so everyone should feel safe asking questions and making mistakes.

- Be kind and patient, especially with first-time contributors.
- Review the code, not the person.
- No harassment, insults or discriminatory jokes, in issues, pull requests, clues or player names.
- Keep clues funny and friendly. No jokes that put down a real group of people.

If something makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer, @prabaltripathiofficial, on GitHub. Maintainers may remove content or block accounts that break these rules.
